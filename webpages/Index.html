History of the

AIRBUS A380

The Airbus A380 is the world's largest passenger aircraft, renowned for its double-deck design, impressive capacity, long-range performance, comfort, and groundbreaking contribution to modern aviation.

A Brief History Of Airbus A380

2000

Programme launched

Airbus begins the very large aircraft project

2005

First flight

The A380 takes off from Toulouse, France

2007

Enters service

Singapore Airlines operates the first commercial flight

2010s

Global flagship

Loved for space and comfort, but costly to operate

2019

Production ends

Falling orders lead Airbus to close the programme

2021 - today

A lasting legacy

Final delivery in 2021; many still fly worldwide
